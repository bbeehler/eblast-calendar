Send rules checkWhat breaks the new rules from today on

Checked against the audit's fixes 1 to 3: two broad sends a week and never two in a day, three sends per reminder series, one partner email a month. Rules took effect Oct 1, 2026.

Each entry is the send task in Wrike, or the eBlast project's end date where no separate send task exists. Click an entry to open it. “Past due” means the send date has passed but the task is still open, which usually means nobody closed it after the send went out.

The calendar refreshes from Wrike every 15 minutes. Whether a send is broad, and its step in its series, comes from the Broad send and Series step fields on the Wrike task when they're filled in. Otherwise it's guessed from the title and marked auto-tagged. Regional golf, CCIF-list sends, KBYG, surveys and sponsor-segment emails are treated as targeted. Newsletters, Driving Change, Auto-Jobs, the Event Calendar and research reports are not tracked as eBlasts in this space. So the weekly totals are a minimum, and a week that looks clear may already have a newsletter in it.
